Customer Complaint & Redress Procedure

If something goes wrong with an order or your experience on the Platform, here's exactly how we handle it:

  1. Submit a complaint — through in-app order support, email to pasugoarakan@gmail.com, or mobile/SMS to 0961 861 0787. Include your order number and a description of the issue.
  2. We acknowledge it — within one (1) business day of receiving your complaint.
  3. We review it — against the order record, typically completed within three (3) business days.
  4. We investigate if needed — contacting the relevant Merchant or Rider for their account of what happened, where the order record alone doesn't clearly establish it.
  5. We determine a resolution — a refund, wallet credit, replacement, or a clear explanation if the complaint isn't substantiated.
  6. You receive the resolution — generally within seven (7) business days of your original complaint.
  7. Unresolved complaints are escalated — to management review if you're not satisfied with the outcome, with a final determination generally within a further seven (7) business days.
