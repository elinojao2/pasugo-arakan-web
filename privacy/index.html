Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy covers all three Pasugo Arakan apps — Customer, Rider, and Merchant — since they're part of one connected delivery platform serving Arakan, North Cotabato, Philippines. It explains what information we collect, why, and how it's protected, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Information we collect

What we collect depends on which app you use and your role on the platform:

  • Account information — full name, email (optional), and mobile number. Your mobile number is verified via a one-time SMS code before you can place orders, accept deliveries, or set up a store.
  • Location data — delivery addresses you save (with GPS coordinates), and for the Rider app specifically, your real-time location while you're online and delivering, so customers and merchants can track their order.
  • Order and transaction history — items ordered, delivery details, amounts paid, payment method chosen (cash, GCash, Maya, or in-app wallet), and your wallet/loyalty point balance.
  • Verification documents — for riders and merchants only: government ID, driver's license, vehicle registration (OR/CR), and business permit, submitted during approval and reviewed by our admin team.
  • Photos — profile pictures, store/product photos, vehicle photos, and delivery proof-of-completion photos, depending on your role.
  • Payout details — for riders and merchants, the GCash number or bank account you provide when requesting a withdrawal.
  • Reviews and ratings — feedback you leave for stores or riders after a delivery.

How we use this information

  • To create and verify your account, and confirm you're a real person with a working mobile number
  • To connect customers, riders, and merchants for order fulfillment — a rider needs your delivery address, a customer needs to know their rider is really on the way
  • To calculate delivery fees based on distance, and to process payments and payouts
  • To review and approve rider and merchant applications
  • To send order updates, OTP codes, and important account notifications via SMS, email, or in-app push notification
  • To improve the platform and investigate reports of fraud, abuse, or safety issues

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. Information is shared only as needed to make the platform work:

  • Between platform participants — e.g., a rider sees the pickup/drop-off address and the customer's contact number for the specific order they're delivering; a merchant sees a customer's order and delivery address to fulfill it.
  • Service providers — our SMS provider (Semaphore) for OTP codes, our email provider (Google) for account emails, and Google Maps for location and routing. These providers only receive what's needed to perform their specific function.
  • Legal requirements — if required by Philippine law or a valid legal request.

Data retention

We keep your account and order data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward for accounting, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Contact below) — some order records may be retained longer where required for financial record-keeping.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to certain uses of it. Contact us using the details below to exercise any of these rights.

Children's privacy

Pasugo Arakan is not directed at children, and account registration requires a verified mobile number and involves financial transactions. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 18.

Security

Your password is stored in encrypted (hashed) form and never stored in plain text. Connections between the app and our servers use HTTPS encryption. Access to sensitive data like verification documents is restricted to authorized admin review.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the apps after a change means you accept the updated policy.
