Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

This policy covers all three Pasugo Arakan apps — Customer, Rider, and Merchant — since they're part of one connected delivery platform serving Arakan, North Cotabato, Philippines. It explains what personal information we collect, why, and how it's protected, in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

1. Information We Collect

What we collect depends on your role on the platform:

  • Customers: full name, mobile number, email address (optional), delivery addresses, order history, payment method preference, and loyalty point balance.
  • Merchants: business name, owner's full name, email, mobile number, store location, business category, product catalog, and bank/GCash details for settlement.
  • Riders: full name, mobile number, email, government-issued ID, driver's license number, vehicle type and plate number, OR/CR, and a selfie photo submitted for identity verification.

We also automatically collect device information, app usage logs, and — where relevant to the service — GPS location data as described below.

2. How We Use Personal Information

  • Creating and managing user accounts across the customer, merchant, and rider apps.
  • Processing and fulfilling orders across all four service types: Store Delivery, Pasabuy, Courier, and Pa Angkas.
  • Verifying mobile numbers via OTP before an order can be placed.
  • Calculating delivery fees, service fees, and merchant commissions.
  • Facilitating payment collection, wallet crediting, and withdrawal requests.
  • Sending order status notifications via push notification, SMS, and/or email.
  • Investigating and resolving customer complaints, disputes, and reports of misconduct.
  • Complying with applicable Philippine laws, including tax and business registration requirements.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

3. GPS and Location Data Collection

  • Customers: location is used, with permission, to show nearby stores and set an accurate delivery pin. Declining location access only limits the "nearby stores" feature.
  • Riders: while online and delivering, the app transmits live GPS coordinates so customers can track their delivery and so arrival at pickup/drop-off can be detected automatically. Transmission stops when a rider goes offline.
  • Merchants: a store's fixed location is used to calculate delivery fees and determine which customers fall within delivery range.

4. Information Shared With Merchants, Riders, and Service Providers

  • A Merchant receives the customer's name, delivery address, order items, and delivery notes needed to prepare and hand off the order.
  • A Rider receives the customer's name, mobile number (for delivery coordination only), and delivery address for their assigned delivery.
  • A Customer receives the assigned rider's name, vehicle type, plate number, and a limited contact method.

We also share limited data with service providers strictly to operate the platform, including our SMS gateway (for OTP and notifications), cloud image hosting, and backend hosting providers — each contractually limited to using data only to provide their service to us. We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Data Security and Retention

We apply reasonable safeguards including encrypted password storage, rate-limited authentication, role-based access controls, and regular backups. Account data is retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period afterward as required for tax and accounting compliance. You may request account deletion at any time; data is removed or anonymized except where retention is legally required.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to be informed of data collection, access your own data, request correction of inaccurate data, object to or withdraw consent for certain processing, request deletion where no longer necessary, be notified of a data breach affecting you, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

7. Data Protection Contact Information

Questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to the contact details below. We aim to respond within fifteen (15) business days. This Policy may be updated from time to time; material changes will be communicated through the app before taking effect.
